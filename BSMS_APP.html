Required before booking

Hair & Scalp Preparation Policy

Please arrive with your hair and scalp clean and free of excessive oil, grease, product buildup, and debris.

If your barber must clean or prepare your hair and/or scalp before your scheduled service can begin, a $5 Hair & Scalp Preparation Fee will be added to your service total.

The $5 fee is not charged merely for agreeing to this policy. It is added only when the barber actually has to perform the extra cleaning or preparation.
